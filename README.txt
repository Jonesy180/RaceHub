OTG! MAIN v8.0.56 — FH5 FESTIVAL / RACE OFF REBUILD

FH5 PRESET REBUILD
- Existing Festival categories retained and supplied with suggested programmes from the final multi-tab FH5 race-setups workbook.
- Adds Rally Adventure, Hot Wheels and Drag Racing specialist Championship families.
- Fresh FH5 setups preload a suggested format and track programme; normal presets remain editable before START.
- Format defaults: 2–16 STANDARD, 17–64 GROUPS, 65+ SWISS.
- Swiss follows the v8.0.55 foundation rule: 3 recommended rounds for 8–64; 4 for 65+; advanced/custom may use more.
- All Cars Festival is the fixed exception: STANDARD / 1 round / Copper Canyon Sprint.
- Race Off uses the same FH5 knowledge to suggest tracks round by round; suggestions remain editable before REVIEW DRAW.
