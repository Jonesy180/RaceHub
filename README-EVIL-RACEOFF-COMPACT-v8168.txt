PURPOSE
Compact the active FH5 EVIL Tournament Race Off progression / draw screen.
- A short UP NEXT box shows the next saved match and both actual car IDs.
- Cyan/orange/purple E✓ badges based on the car's MOST RECENT recorded Evil event.
